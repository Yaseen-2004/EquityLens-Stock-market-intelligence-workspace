Risk against return

Annualised volatility on the horizontal axis, annualised growth on the vertical. Up and to the left is better: more return for less risk. Anything above the dashed line beat the {{ a.benchmark }} benchmark.

Annualised return (CAGR)

The constant yearly rate that turns the starting price into the ending price. It is the honest way to compare periods, unlike a raw total.

Growth of $100

An equal-weight basket of all {{ a.rows|length }}, rebalanced daily, against the market. Both start at $100 on the first day.

Worst peak-to-trough fall

How far each fell from its own running high — the loss an investor would actually have lived through, not the fall from the best price.

Correlation of daily returns

1.00 means they move together every day; 0.00 means no relationship. Darker is more correlated — and less diversifying.
