How this was measured

  • Walk-forward. At each step the models saw only prices up to that day, predicted {{ f.horizon }} days ahead, and were scored against what actually happened. Nothing is scored on data it was fitted to.
  • No lookahead. Training rows are restricted to those whose outcome had already occurred by the prediction date. Getting this wrong is the classic way to build a forecaster that looks brilliant in a backtest and useless in reality.
  • The band comes from volatility, widening with the square root of time — the standard random-walk assumption. It is not a model output, and it does not narrow because a model feels confident.
  • One period, ten companies. That is a small sample. Differences of a few hundredths of a skill point mean nothing.
